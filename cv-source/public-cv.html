Research Profile

Fascinated by the fundamental physics of charge transport, I am a dedicated organic electronics researcher specializing in the molecular design and microfabrication of high-performance devices. I possess deep expertise in small-molecule synthesis and in addressing the critical stability and transport bottlenecks in n-channel organic field-effect transistors (OFETs). A proven project leader with multiple corresponding-author publications, I leverage strong national and international collaborations to bridge the gap between fundamental chemistry and efficient electronic applications.
